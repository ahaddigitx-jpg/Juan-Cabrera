02 / Positioning

Your current positioning

The profile establishes your role, company, broad industry reach, and authorship. The next step is making the reason to care more immediate.

What comes through

Founder with technical range and a wide SaaS portfolio

The supplied profile connects SoftWorks Global with SaaS, small business operations, AI, cloud technology, and automation. The company website adds a clear point of difference: focused products built for specific industry workflows.

What can be clearer

Who benefits, and why this approach is different

A visitor should quickly understand that you build specialized software for small business operators, rather than a single generic platform. The industry breadth is proof of the model, not the whole message.

Current headline

Founder & CEO at SoftWorks Global | Building SaaS software for small business operations across 100+ industries | Author of Smart AI Agents

A visitor arriving from a post, referral, or company page can identify your role and the broad category. They still have to infer what makes the portfolio distinctive. Also, the profile says “100+ industries,” while the company website states “100+ SaaS platforms” and “15+ industries.” Aligning this language avoids confusion.

04 / About

Make the About section work harder

Your current About has strong ingredients: mission, technical range, and a commitment to practical software. It repeats the mission in a few places and delays the clearest differentiation. The replacement leads with the buyer's reality, explains the vertical SaaS model, and uses public portfolio proof.
